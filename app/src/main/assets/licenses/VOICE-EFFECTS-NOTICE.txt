Call voice-effects references and adaptations

Basic Call Player (BCP), chenxiaolong, GPL-3.0
https://github.com/chenxiaolong/BCP/tree/57977e5526b6ad3983d7db9c8a64236e6f1eff15
TelephonyPcmOutput adapts the AudioTrack construction/routing in PlayerThread.kt.
File decoding is not included. The output is fed live PCM by this app instead.
BCP does not provide the microphone-replacement or downlink-relay mechanism.
See BCP-GPL-3.0.txt and the project LICENSE for license terms.
